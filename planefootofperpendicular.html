Finding Foot of Perpendicular from a point P to a plane

📋 General Method

Step 1

Vector Equation of Line

Form a line passing through \(P\) with direction vector \(\mathbf{n}\). State OF \( = \vec{OP} + \lambda \mathbf{n}\).

Step 2

Substitute into Plane

Substitute OF into the scalar product form of the plane: \( \vec{OF} \cdot \mathbf{n} = d \).

Step 3

Solve for parameter

Expand the dot product and solve the resulting equation for \(\lambda\).

Step 4

Find the Foot

Substitute \(\lambda\) back into Step 1 to find the foot of perpendicular of the point P to the plane.
