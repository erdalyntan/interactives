📋 General Method

Let \(\vec{OF}\) be the position vector of the foot of perpendicular from a point \(P\) to a line \(\mathit{l}\).

Step 1

Vector Equation of Line

State \(\vec{OF} = \mathbf{a} + \lambda \mathbf{d}\) for some \(\lambda \in \mathbb{R}\).

Step 2

Express \(\vec{PF}\)

Find displacement: \(\vec{PF} = \vec{OF} - \vec{OP}\).

Step 3

Perpendicular Condition

Set the dot product to zero: \(\vec{PF} \cdot \mathbf{d} = 0\).

Step 4

Calculate Foot \(\vec{OF}\)

Solve for \(\lambda\) and substitute back to find final coordinates.
