Finding the Foot of Perpendicular from a Point to a Line

📋 General Method

Let OF be the position vector of the foot of perpendicular from a point \(P\) to a line \(\mathit{l}\).

Step 1

Vector Equation of Line

State OF \( = \mathbf{a} + \lambda \mathbf{d}\) for some \(\lambda \in \mathbb{R}\).

Step 2

Express PF

Find displacement: PF \( = \) OF \( - \) OP.

Step 3

Perpendicular Condition

Since PF \( \perp \mathit{l}\), set the dot product to zero: PF \( \cdot \mathbf{d} = 0\).

Step 4

Calculate Foot OF

Solve for \(\lambda\) and substitute back to find final coordinates.
